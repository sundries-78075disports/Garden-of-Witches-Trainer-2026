## 🎮 About the Game

Garden of Witches is a fast-paced 2D isometric action roguelike developed and published by Team Tapas. It launched in full on August 28, 2026, following its Early Access release on May 23, 2025. Players control Sil, the Thread Witch, using giant scissors and witchcraft to confront rival witches and save the Garden.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Garden of Witches**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for Garden of Witches runs. It supports Sil's combat systems, including Scissors, Spells, Special Skills, Imprints, Relics, charging, combos, channeling, and movement through the Garden.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with different Scissors, Spells, Special Skills, Imprints, and Relic combinations, reduce repetition during runs, or make story and Challenge Mode sessions more accessible.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the movement controls to navigate each Garden and reposition during battles. Movement Speed can be adjusted for quicker traversal, while Instant Rush Recovery supports more frequent evasive movement when Rush is part of the active build.

### Tools & Inventory

Garden of Witches focuses on loadouts rather than a conventional item inventory. Use the Armory to select unlocked Scissors, Spells, and Special Skills. During progression, Imprints and Relics shape the build by modifying individual abilities and creating interactions between skills.

### World & Environment

Each run takes Sil through Gardens connected to the personalities and stories of different witches. Progress through chapters and epilogues to unlock additional abilities and content. Challenge Mode becomes available after clearing Epilogue 3 and provides tougher tests for established builds.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat revolves around giant Scissors, witchcraft, and enemy attack patterns. Supported abilities may use charging, combos, or channeling, while Relics and Imprints determine how a build develops. Activate trainer options selectively to test damage output, practice encounters, or reduce the difficulty of repeated runs.
